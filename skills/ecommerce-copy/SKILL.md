# E-commerce copy that sounds human

## Process

1. **Mine customer words**: reviews (yours + competitors' 3-star reviews), support emails, return reasons, search terms. Copy their phrases into a list.
2. **Pick the job**: click, explain, reassure, justify price, or prompt action. One job per block.
3. **Draft with specifics**: numbers, materials, situations, ages, times.
4. **Cut**: remove every word that doesn't add a fact or a feeling. Then cut 20% more.
5. **AI-tell scan**: check against the banned list below. Rewrite any hit.
6. **Read aloud**.

## Templates

**Product description**
```
[Hook: main benefit in the customer's words - 1 sentence]

- **[Benefit 1]** - [fact that proves it]
- **[Benefit 2]** - [fact]
- **[Benefit 3]** - [fact]

[Optional: 2-3 sentences of context - who it's for, when you use it, why we made it]

[Specs table - from metafields]
```

**Example (FR-CA)**
> Le casque que ton enfant acceptera de porter : léger, ventilé, et il s'ajuste d'une seule main.
> - **Seulement 280 g** - plus léger qu'une boîte à lunch pleine.
> - **Molette d'ajustement** - de 48 à 54 cm, sans retirer le casque.
> - **Visibilité le soir** - lumière DEL arrière intégrée, rechargeable par USB-C.

(Only use facts that are true for the actual product.)

**Headlines**: [Outcome] + [for whom/when]. Or a plain statement of what's sold. Or the customer's own phrase.

**CTA**: verb + object: "Ajouter au panier", "Voir les manteaux 2-6 ans", "Choisir ma taille".

**Email subject**: specific, ≤ 45 characters, no clickbait, no ALL CAPS.

## Banned phrases (AI tells)

**English**: elevate, unlock, unleash, seamless(ly), effortless(ly), game-changer, revolutionize, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, curated, delve, embark, journey, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, robust, leverage, empower, supercharge, next-level, look no further, in today's fast-paced world, whether you're X or Y, not just X - it's Y, say goodbye to, say hello to, introducing, discover the magic, perfect for any occasion, must-have, best-in-class.

**Français**: sublimer, révolutionner, incontournable, inégalé(e), sans pareil, un véritable allié, votre meilleur allié, plongez dans, découvrez l'univers de, laissez-vous séduire, alliant X et Y, que vous soyez X ou Y, bien plus qu'un simple, plus qu'un X, un Y, n'attendez plus, à la pointe, haut de gamme (without proof), au quotidien (as filler), dites adieu à, le must-have, l'alliance parfaite, conçu pour vous accompagner.

**Structures to avoid**: rhetorical question openers ("Vous cherchez… ?"), lists of exactly three adjectives, em-dash chains, exclamation marks in every sentence, emoji bullets ✨🚀, Title Case Headlines, closing summary sentences that repeat the bullets.

## Quebec French

- Native writing, not translation. OQLF terms: courriel, magasiner, panier, clavarder, fin de semaine, infolettre, livraison gratuite.
- Formats: `49,99 $` · `1 299 $` · `40 °C` · `14 h 30` · « guillemets ».
- Choose tu or vous once per brand; keep it everywhere (site, emails, ads).
- Bill 96: French content required for Quebec commercial content, at least as prominent as other languages.

## Legal/ethics

No invented reviews, statistics, or certifications. "Best/#1" only with proof. Real deadlines and real stock only. Compare-at prices only if real.
